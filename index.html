Utku Okur

I am a postdoc at the Institute of Discrete Mathematics and Algebra at TU Bergakademie Freiberg, Germany. I work with Prof. Johannes Carmesin. I am involved in various projects on combinatorics and Lean 4 benchmarks for AI.

I received my PhD in mathematics from the University of South Carolina (USA), advised by Prof. Joshua Cooper (thesis: Coefficients of the characteristic polynomial of a hypergraph and their combinatorial properties). I completed my master's in mathematics at Bilkent University (Turkey) with Prof. Laurence Barker (thesis: Mackey decomposition for Brauer pairs). I hold a BSc in mathematics and a BA in philosophy from Boğaziçi University.
