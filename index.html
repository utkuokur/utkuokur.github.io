Utku Okur

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Discrete Structures group of Johannes Carmesin at TU Bergakademie Freiberg, Germany. I work in algebraic and enumerative combinatorics (spectra of hypergraphs, Eulerian graphs and their cycle partitions, reconstruction problems for trees) and on formal verification with Lean 4 and certified SAT solving.

I received my PhD in mathematics from the University of South Carolina, advised by Joshua Cooper. Before that I completed an MSc in mathematics at Bilkent University with Laurence Barker (thesis: Mackey decomposition for Brauer pairs), and a BSc in mathematics and a BA in philosophy at Boğaziçi University.
